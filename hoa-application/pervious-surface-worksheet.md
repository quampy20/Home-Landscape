## Total-lot calculation

```
Total non-pervious = House footprint + Existing flatwork (D) + New pool/deck
                   = 2,301 + D + 720
                   = 3,021 + D

Percentage        = (3,021 + D) / 5,565

75% limit         = 4,174 sf  →  D must be ≤ 1,153 sf
```

### ⚠ Heads-up — this is worth measuring carefully

A typical 3-car driveway (~550–650 sf) plus the existing rear walkway and front flatwork
could put **D in the 900–1,150 sf range**, which lands the total **near the 75% cap**.
Measure the existing flatwork before submitting. If the total exceeds 75%:

- Reduce the deck footprint (e.g., 3' deck on the fence side but narrower aprons at the
  ends trims 60–100 sf), or
- Use permeable pavers for a portion of the deck (counts as pervious), or
- Confirm with Landmark Limited whether pool water surface may be excluded.
