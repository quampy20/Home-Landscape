# Landscape Plan — Plant Locations & Species

**Property:** 12344 Passerine Court, Rancho Cordova, CA — Lot 46, The Ranch Village 2
**Scope:** Backyard only (53' × 25'). Plant locations are shown on the attached `site-plan.png`.
**Climate:** USDA Zone 9b / Sunset Zone 14 (Sacramento area). All selections are Zone 9 appropriate.

## Planting Areas

### Area A — Privacy screen, 3' setback strip along back fence
Runs the width of the yard between the rear fence and the pool deck. Plants sit inside the
3' HOA setback strip; the strip is landscape bed (pervious).

| Qty | Plant | Botanical name | Size at install | Spacing | Mature size (maintained) |
|-----|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|--------------------------|
| 12 | Fern Pine (columnar screen) | *Podocarpus gracilior* | 15-gal | 4' on center | 8–10' H × 3–4' W (pruned) |

*Alternate if preferred: Photinia × fraseri or Green Giant Arborvitae at the same spacing.
Podocarpus is recommended near pools — clean, low-litter, evergreen, takes shearing well.*

### Area B — Accent color pockets flanking the pool deck (east & west sides)
Four planting pockets in the open yard beside the deck, two per side.

| Qty | Plant | Botanical name | Size at install | Spacing | Bloom |
|-----|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|-------|
| 8 | Kangaroo Paw | *Anigozanthos* 'Bush Ranger' / flavidus hybrids | 1-gal | 30" o.c. | Red/orange spikes, spring–fall |
| 6 | Autumn Sage | *Salvia greggii* (red & pink) | 1-gal | 30" o.c. | Spring–fall, hummingbird plant |
| 6 | Lantana 'New Gold' | *Lantana × hybrida* | 1-gal | 3' o.c. | Gold, late spring–frost |
| 6 | Dwarf Mat Rush | *Lomandra longifolia* 'Breeze' | 1-gal | 30" o.c. | Evergreen grass texture |

### Area C — Remaining open yard
Balance of yard outside deck and beds: decomposed granite or river-rock ground cover over
landscape fabric (pervious), or drought-tolerant no-mow fescue. Final choice noted on application.

## Materials

- **Bed mulch:** 3" shredded bark or ¾" crushed rock over soil (pervious)
- **Bed edging:** steel or concrete mow strip between beds and open yard
- **Irrigation:** automatic drip system to all plantings (point-source emitters), per
  Rancho Cordova Municipal Code 23.716.060 water-efficient landscape provisions

## Notes for the Committee

- All plantings are behind the wing fences (backyard) — no front-yard changes
- Privacy screen is maintained at or below fence-adjacent shrub norms (8–10') and set 3' off the fence
- No plantings encroach on the P.U.E. shown on the recorded plot plan
- Landscape beds and rock/DG areas are pervious surfaces and are included as such in the
  attached pervious-surface worksheet
