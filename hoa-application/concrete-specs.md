# Proposed Concrete — Color, Finish, Dimensions, Setbacks & Drainage

**Property:** 12344 Passerine Court, Rancho Cordova, CA — Lot 46

## Scope of Concrete Work

New pool deck surrounding the fiberglass pool shell, tying into the existing rear walkway/patio.

| Item | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| Overall deck footprint | 36' × 20' (720 sf gross) |
| Pool shell cut-out within deck | approx. 380 sf (Sierra Bermuda, 14' × 30' freeform) |
| Net new concrete flatwork | approx. 340 sf |
| Thickness | 4" over compacted aggregate base (per pool contractor engineering) |
| Color | Natural gray, to match existing builder walkway/patio |
| Finish | Medium broom finish (slip-resistant), matching existing flatwork |
| Coping | Pre-cast concrete coping at pool edge, buff/cream tone |
| Joints | Tooled control joints on nominal 9' grid |

*A physical color/finish sample chip from the concrete contractor accompanies this application.*

| Direction | Distance from property line to concrete edge |
|-----------|----------------------------------------------|

| Side (east) | 8' 6" |

Pool water edge is a minimum of 6' 0" from the rear property line (3' setback + 3' deck).

## Drainage Statement

- The existing drainage pattern is **not altered**. Roof downspouts remain connected to the
  underground drain lines installed by the builder (per approved plot plan note), and overall
  lot grading/flow direction is unchanged.
- The new deck is finished at grade and sloped a minimum of 2% (¼" per foot) **away from the
  pool and away from the house** onto adjacent pervious landscape area.
- The 3' rear setback strip remains a pervious planted bed, so no runoff is directed at the
  rear or side fences/neighboring lots.
- Deck drains (if required by the pool contractor's plan) will discharge to the existing
  yard drainage system, not onto neighboring property.

*To be verified against the pool contractor's final engineering/grading plan before construction.*
