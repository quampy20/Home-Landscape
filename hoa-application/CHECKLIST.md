## ⬜ You must provide

- [ ] **Print and sign the official application form** (name, phone, dates, owner signature) —
      use `application-draft.md` to fill it in
- [ ] **Neighbor acknowledgment signatures** (Lots 45 & 47 and rear neighbors: name,
      address, signature, phone, date on the form's table)
- [ ] **Measure existing flatwork** (driveway, front walk, rear walkway) and complete the
      blanks in `pervious-surface-worksheet.md` — ⚠ the total is likely close to the 75%
      cap, so measure before submitting
- [ ] **Concrete color/finish sample chip** from your pool/concrete contractor
- [ ] **City of Rancho Cordova building permit** — pools require one; check whether the HOA
      wants the permit copy with the application or after issuance
- [ ] **Photos of any work already completed** (only if any work has been started — otherwise N/A)
- [ ] **Mail or email the complete package** to Landmark Limited (keep a copy; follow up to
      confirm receipt; allow up to 45 days)
